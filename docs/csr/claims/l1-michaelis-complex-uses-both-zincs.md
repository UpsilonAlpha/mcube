# In the only measured B3 Michaelis complex, the substrate is held by both zincs

7UHI/7UHJ, L1 with **intact** moxalactam, are the only crystallographic
Michaelis complexes in the family - no MBL has ever been crystallised with an
intact beta-lactam except L1 (¶158).

What they measure:

- the intact carbonyl at **2.53/2.63 A** from **Zn1**;
- the ring nitrogen and the C-carboxylate on **Zn2**, at 2.65/2.67 A.

And in the product-end template 5AXO (SMB-1 with hydrolysed meropenem) the new
carboxylate O32 and N4 are **both** on Zn2, at 2.26 and 2.37 A.

**Why this is the sharpest argument in the map.** The part of the pose nearest
the chemistry is anchored by a metal CSR-1 does not have, and no amount of fold
similarity changes that (¶158) - CSR-1 is in fact the closest B3 to L1 of the
three compared, 39.0% identity and 1.05 A core CA RMSD over 221 residues, which
makes the point harder rather than softer. So the templates can place a
substrate in CSR-1's site, and cannot say what holds it there.

¶156 is the measurement that matters for our own structures: the docked pose in
use since ¶123 puts the lactam carbonyl **4.90 A** from the zinc where L1's
transferred ligand puts it at 2.27, and the C3 carboxylate at 8.14 A where L1's
is 2.82.
