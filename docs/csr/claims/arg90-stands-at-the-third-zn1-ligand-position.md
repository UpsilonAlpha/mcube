# Arg90 sits at the third Zn1 ligand position, 4.02 A from the metal and not coordinating

6NC5, the only CSR-1 crystal with a metal in it: **Arg118 NH1 is 4.02 A** from
ZN302, and that zinc's four ligands are His87 NE2 2.22, His163 NE2 2.09,
HOH485 1.90, HOH493 2.14 - **two** protein ligands where canonical B3 has three
(¶22, ¶39, ¶108).

Arg90 is BBL **Arg118**, which is a Zn1 ligand position in canonical B3
(His116, His118, His196). So the substitution is at a metal-ligand position and
the residue there does not ligate the metal.

**No published structure has an Arg coordinating a zinc in this family**, and
Krco 2023 argues that arginines and lysines at these positions are protonated
at catalytic pH and are poor metal ligands (¶22). The user's earlier
understanding that the zinc is coordinated by Arg118 plus two His is recorded in
¶22 as unconfirmed, and the crystal does not support it.
