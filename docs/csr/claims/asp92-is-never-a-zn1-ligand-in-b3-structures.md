# The conserved aspartate is never a Zn1 ligand in any B3 structure

Measured directly in 1SML, 5K0W, 7UHI, 5AXO and 6NC5: in **every** one the
conserved aspartate (BBL Asp120, CSR-1 Asp92) is a **Zn2** ligand, and it is
never within bonding distance of Zn1. A Zn-O bond is 1.9-2.2 A; nothing here
is.

This is the project's stated ground truth (¶206 rule 1, user: "asp is not
coordinated in the crystal or in any other B3 structure - that's our ground
truth"). It is the qualitative statement, and it is the part that survives
every reading of the numbers.

**What this observation does NOT establish** is any particular Zn1-Asp92
distance for CSR-1. See [[asp-zn1-distances-are-set-by-the-zn2-ion]] and
[[question-how-far-is-asp92-from-zn1-in-wild-type-csr1]]. ¶206's own
correction block is the record of that error being made inside the entry that
established the rule.

One caveat travels with it, from ¶208: the stronger form "no B3 enzyme has
Asp120 on Zn1" is a generalisation over the subclass, and what is measured is
five structures. That is enough for the decision and not enough for the paper
without a source.
