# CSR-1's single zinc sits at Zn1 - the position every other mono-zinc MBL leaves empty

The modelled metal is 6NC5's **ZN302**, whose own ligands His87/His163 are not
mutated in that construct, four-coordinate at His87 NE2 2.22, His163 NE2 2.09,
HOH485 1.90, HOH493 2.14 (D108, re-measured in D208). That is the H-R-H Zn1
site (BBL H116/H196).

**This is the structural reason no published mono-zinc mechanism transfers.**
Every mononuclear MBL in the literature carries its metal at **Zn2** - GOB-18,
Sfh-I, B2 CphA - which is the site that stabilises the anionic nitrogen. CSR-1
is the only mono-zinc MBL found whose metal sits where the nucleophilic
hydroxide normally lives (D124). A scheme built for a Zn2-site enzyme cannot be
carried over wholesale (D107).
