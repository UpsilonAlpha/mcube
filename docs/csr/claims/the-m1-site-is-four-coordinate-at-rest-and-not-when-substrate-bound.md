# Crystallographic coordination number runs 3 to 6; four is the resting M1 value, and substrate-bound sites are not four

Every crystallographic zinc with at least one histidine ligand, counted at this
project's own 2.6 Å cutoff and again at 3.0 Å. Internal distances only.

| structure | site | occ | CN 2.6 | CN 3.0 | donors |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 6NC5 ZN302 | M1 | 1.00 | **4** | 4 | H,H + 2 waters |
| 1SML ZN271 | M1 | 1.00 | **4** | 4 | H,H,H + bridge |
| 5AXO ZN301 | M1 | 1.00 | **4** | 4 | H,H,H + 1 water |
| 6NC5 ZN301 | M2 | 1.00 | 4 | 4 | D,H,H + 1 water |
| 1SML ZN270 | M2 | 1.00 | 5 | 5 | D,H,H + 2 waters |
| refine_16 ZN2 | M1 | 0.53 | 5 | 6 | H,H + 4 waters |
| 6DQH ZN401 | M1 | 1.00 | **6** | 6 | H,H,H + 2 waters + PO4 |
| refine_12 ZN1 | M2 | 0.70 | **6** | 6 | D,H,H + 3 waters |
| refine_16 ZN1 | M2 | 1.00 | **6** | 6 | D,H,H + 3 waters |
| 5AXO ZN302 **+ligand** | M2 | 1.00 | **6** | 6 | D,H,H + bridge + ligand O,N |
| 7UHI ZN301 **Michaelis** | M1 | 0.49 | **3** | 4 | H,H + ligand O1 2.53 |
| 7UHI ZN302 **Michaelis** | M2 | 0.77 | **3** | 5 | D,H,H + ligand O8, N1 |

**Two findings, and they pull in opposite directions.**

**Four-coordinate is the resting M1 value**, agreed by three independent
structures. 6NC5's M1 is two histidines plus two waters - the same sphere ¶120
describes for ours - and L1's and 5AXO's are three histidines plus one water.
So that premise is a crystal value, not an assumption.

**It is not a property of MBL zinc sites generally, and specifically not of
substrate-bound ones.** M2 is five or six almost everywhere. And the two sites
that actually hold a substrate go the other way: **5AXO's ligand-bound M2 is
six**, and **7UHI's M1 - the one real Michaelis complex of this enzyme class -
is three at 2.6 Å, with the substrate oxygen at 2.53 and no water at all**
([[the-l1-michaelis-complex-has-no-metal-bound-water]]).

So a [4,5] window describes a **resting** site and would reject 7UHI's own
arrangement if applied to a reactant.
