# Lys226 occupies the vacant Zn2 metal-ligand position

Lys226 is BBL **Lys263**, a Zn2 metal-ligand position where canonical B3 has a
histidine - confirmed by the 6DR8 mutant R118H/Q121H/**K254H**, which restores
exactly that histidine (¶107). It is **not** BBL Lys224, the B1 carboxylate
anchor; B3 enzymes have Ser221/Ser223 there, and ¶12/¶13's reading of it as a
carboxylate lysine is retracted.

Lys226 does salt-bridge the substrate's C3 carboxylate in our trajectories, but
for a different reason, and not persistently: 2.61 A at closest in `nacqm`,
opening to 4.26 A over the next 50 ps with no other cation taking over
(¶107 as qualified by ¶111).
