# In the B3-RQK sibling SPR-1, activity was only observed for the N-terminally truncated construct, and CSR-1's two constructs are indistinguishable at the site

Read 2026-11-06, not re-derived.

- **SPR-1** (*Serratia proteamaculans*, the other characterised B3-RQK enzyme,
  and the one sometimes proposed as a subclass **B4**): beta-lactamase activity
  was observed only when the protein was **truncated by 49 residues at the
  N-terminus**. SPR-1 also has **no crystal structure**, so its zinc ligands and
  binding mode are unconfirmed - which is why CSR-1's deposits carry the
  structural argument for the whole RQK group.
- **CSR-1**: the paper reports **no metal ions in the crystal structures of
  either CSR-1 or CSR-1_trunc**. The two constructs are therefore not
  distinguished by anything observable at the active site.
- SPR-1 is also described as possibly binding **one Zn(II) in a resting,
  catalytically inactive form**, which is an independent literature statement
  that an RQK enzyme's resting state is mono-zinc
  ([[csr1-is-mono-zinc-with-zn2-vacant]]).

**What this supports and what it does not.** It supports modelling the truncated
construct: it is the form shown to be active in the sibling enzyme, and nothing
at CSR-1's site differs between the two. It does **not** establish that
truncation is catalytically neutral - an activity difference consistent with
expression or solubility is also consistent with catalysis, and no source read so
far separates them.
