# Both wild-type CSR-1 structures are apo: zero metal atoms

Measured, not inferred (¶208): **6DN4** 4557 atoms, **zero** metal sites;
**6DQ2** 4049 atoms, **zero** metal sites. 6DN4 is the mature wild type at
1.99 A, 6DQ2 the S60 mutant.

Pedroso 2020 attributes the apo crystals to reduced metal affinity, since
Arg118 and Lys254 are protonated at optimal pH (¶40) - so the wild-type
crystals are metal-free because of affinity and crystallisation conditions, not
because the Zn1 site is absent (¶40 as corrected by ¶41: the zinc position we
model **is** crystallographic, it just comes from the mutant's coordinates).

**Two consequences that bite.** Our mono-zinc Zn1 site is an inference from a
mutant's coordinates and must be stated as such. And no wild-type CSR-1
structure can measure anything metal-relative, which is why the Asp92-to-Zn1
distance is unobtainable from the record
([[question-how-far-is-asp92-from-zn1-in-wild-type-csr1]]) and why ¶184's
superposition of 6NC5's zinc into 6DN4 produced a number rather than a
measurement.
