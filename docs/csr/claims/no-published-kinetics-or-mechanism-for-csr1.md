# There is no published mechanistic study of CSR-1 and no published meropenem kinetics

CSR-1 is published as characterisation only: Pedroso MM *et al.*, "Broad
spectrum antibiotic-degrading metallo-beta-lactamases are phylogenetically
diverse", *Protein Cell* 2020;11:613-617, PMID 32542533 (¶22, ¶40). No
substrate or product complex has been deposited for it. No mechanistic study
exists.

**Negative-space support for the thesis.** Nothing published asserts a
canonical mechanism *for this enzyme*, so the thesis is not contradicting a
standing result - it is filling an empty slot. That is weaker than positive
evidence and is recorded here as such: this node makes the thesis *available*,
it does not make it *true*.

**Its real function in the map** is to fix why the computation has to stand
alone. With no published kinetics and the user's own measurements withheld
([[mutant-and-double-mutant-kinetics]]), there is nothing to tune a barrier
against, which is exactly the condition the blind-prediction protocol (¶178)
exists to protect.
