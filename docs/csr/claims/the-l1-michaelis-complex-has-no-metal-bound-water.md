# In the only measured B3 Michaelis complex, neither zinc carries a water

Measured in 7UHI (L1 + **non-hydrolysed** moxalactam, 40 ms time-resolved),
internal distances only:

- **no water within 4.0 A of either zinc**;
- nothing within 3.6 A of the electrophilic carbon C1 except substrate atoms
  and the two metals (Zn1 2.88, Zn2 3.40);
- **Zn1-Zn2 3.17 A with nothing bridging them.**

The substrate occupies the position the bridging hydroxide holds in the resting
enzyme. Taken at face value that means the nucleophile is **not pre-positioned
on the metal** in the Michaelis complex, which is a wrinkle in the canonical
di-zinc-hydroxide picture
([[canonical-b3-mechanism-is-dizinc-hydroxide]]).

**Deliberately weighted as moderate, not decisive.** The deposit is 2.20 A,
Rfree 0.266, with 53 waters modelled. A metal-bound water would normally be
modelled at that resolution - but a time-resolved, partial-occupancy series is
precisely the case where one is missed, and **an absence of modelled density is
not an absence of the atom.** The CSR-1 manuscript supplies this project's own
reminder of the distinction: its text reports no substrate density in a
co-crystallised dataset while its first author's comment records "there is
density there" that could not be fitted
([[dmcsr1-zn1-occupancy-may-depend-on-substrate]]).

So this node does **not** refute the canonical mechanism; it records that the
one experimental Michaelis complex does not show the nucleophile where the
mechanism needs it, and that the gap is a modelling question as much as a
chemical one.
