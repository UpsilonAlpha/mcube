# CSR-1's wild-type mechanism is different from the canonical B3 metallo-beta-lactamase mechanism

**No longer the root of the map.** On 2026-11-06 the project sharpened its
argument to a specific mechanism - that Lys226 restrains Asp92 in place of the
missing Zn2 - and that is now the thesis
([[thesis-lys226-restrains-asp92-in-place-of-zn2]]). This node is kept as a
claim because it is still held and is still what the broader structural evidence
supports: it is what the new thesis *entails*, not what it replaces. Its own
premises below remain the premises of the argument.

Nothing here is retracted. The change is that "different from canonical B3" is a
negative claim and cannot be the content of a paper on its own; the positive
claim about the lysine can.

**Status is `open`, deliberately.** No reaction coordinate has ever been
computed in this project ([[no-reaction-coordinate-has-ever-been-computed]]),
so nothing here is a computed mechanism. What the map can show is the premise
tree: the structural claims that make a canonical mechanism unavailable to this
enzyme, and the candidate replacements with the evidence each actually has.

The structural argument, in one line: the canonical B3 mechanism needs two
zincs and a Zn1-bound bridging hydroxide
([[canonical-b3-mechanism-is-dizinc-hydroxide]]); CSR-1 has one zinc, it sits
at Zn1 ([[csr1-zinc-sits-at-zn1]]), the Zn2 site is vacant with a lysine in it
([[csr1-is-mono-zinc-with-zn2-vacant]]), and the third Zn1 histidine is an
arginine ([[csr1-zn1-site-is-h-r-h]]). Each of those is measured in a deposited
structure. What replaces the missing half is not settled.

**The blind-prediction protocol (D178) applies to this node.** The kinetics
exist, are withheld, and the computed mechanism goes in the log before they are
released. [[mutant-and-double-mutant-kinetics]] is named here as a support node
and is deliberately unread.
