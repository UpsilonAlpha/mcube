# 5K0W has four zincs, both sites occupied in both chains - so "GOB-18's single zinc sits at Zn2" is not what the deposit shows

Measured in D208 as a by-product of resolving the Asp120 contradiction:
**5K0W contains four zinc atoms, with both sites occupied in both chains.**

The project has repeated "GOB-18's single zinc sits in the Zn2 site" since D40,
and used it as the structural premise for two different arguments: that the only
published mono-zinc B3 mechanism belongs to a Zn2-site enzyme (D107), and that
every mononuclear MBL in the literature is a Zn2-site enzyme (D124). **That
sentence needs correcting wherever it appears.**

**What it does and does not undermine.** The GOB-18 *mechanism* paper (Lisa
2017, Nat Commun 8:538) may still be right about the mononuclear solution
species - a crystal grown with excess zinc says little about occupancy in
solution, and the paper's own mechanistic claim is not a claim about 5K0W's
occupancy. What is refuted is the use of the *structure* as evidence for it.
Under D206 that is the difference between a measurement and a reading.

**Also measured in the same pass:** GOB-18's Zn1 is confirmed as the HQH site,
Gln98 OE1 at 2.04 A - D124's point measured rather than read.
