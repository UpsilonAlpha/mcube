# Our single metal sits between the two canonical metal positions, and on top of the canonical nucleophile position

Canonical metals carried into our frame by the CA superpositions of
[[ser186-is-the-canonical-carboxylate-anchor]]:

    L1  (7UHI) Zn1  **1.87 Å** from our Zn      Zn2  **1.77 Å**     (Zn1-Zn2 = 3.17 Å)
    SMB-1 (5B15) Zn1  1.76 Å                      Zn2    2.04 Å

**Our metal is 1.8-2.0 Å from both members of a pair only 3.17 Å apart: it is
between them.** Two things follow.

**1. The Zn1/Zn2 label is about ligands, not position.** At this geometry the
difference between the two sites is smaller than the spread of the fit, which
is the quantitative form of the user's point (2026-11-14) that the Zn2/Zn1
distinction does not matter and what matters is what the metal binds. The
statement "CSR-1's metal is at Zn1" remains true as a statement about its donor
set (His88/His164, [[csr1-zn1-site-is-h-r-h]]); it is not a statement about
where in the site the ion sits.

**2. The canonical nucleophile position is where our metal is.** 5B15's bridging
water, carried across the same fit, lands **1.05 Å from our zinc** (His88 NE2
3.01, His164 NE2 2.51 from that point). A mono-zinc site that has moved into the
bridging position cannot also hold the bridging water, so the nucleophile has to
go elsewhere - which is why ¶283's free-position search was the right question
and why its answer has **no crystallographic precedent in this family**.
That is the weakest link in the proposed mechanism
([[a-free-zinc-site-puts-a-hydroxide-263-angstroms-over-the-scissile-carbon]]).

**3. It makes part of the pose's carboxylate contact a transfer artefact.** The
pose came from 7UHI, where O8 coordinates **Zn2** at 2.65 Å; our metal is 1.77 Å
from that Zn2, so O8 arriving at 1.847 Å from our metal follows from the
transfer. Carrying MX0 across by a global CA fit instead of ¶156's four-atom fit
moves it by mean **1.03 Å** (max 1.44) and gives Zn-O8 **2.75** / Zn-O1 3.82
against the stored 1.847 / 3.153 - the same arrangement, a looser metal contact.
See [[the-crystal-derived-pose-puts-the-carboxylate-on-the-metal]].
