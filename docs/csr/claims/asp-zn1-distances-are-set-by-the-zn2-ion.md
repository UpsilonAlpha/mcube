# The 3.94-4.20 A aspartate-to-Zn1 distances are a consequence of a Zn2 ion holding the carboxylate

The measured Asp-to-Zn1 distances across the B3 references cluster tightly:
6NC5 **4.20** A (OD2) / 4.22 (OD1), 1SML 4.08, 5K0W 4.17, 5B15 4.05, 7UHJ
4.02, and 3.94-4.20 A over the wider set measured since. 6NC5's 4.20 A sits
within 0.03-0.18 A of every other one, so CSR-1's crystal is completely
ordinary for a B3 at this contact (¶208).

**But the number is not free to transfer.** In every one of those structures
the carboxylate is held at 1.95-2.27 A by a **Zn2 ion**
([[asp92-is-a-zn2-ligand-in-b3-structures]]). The 4 A figure is the geometric
consequence of that: it measures the Zn1-Zn2 separation plus the carboxylate's
bite, not an independent preference of the side chain. Remove Zn2 and nothing
in these files says where the carboxylate goes.

6NC5 is itself the Q93H/K226H double mutant **with Zn2 present**, holding that
carboxylate at 1.95 A (¶151, confirmed in ¶208), so it cannot show what the
residue does with Zn2 empty - and that caveat has to travel with its 4.20 A
wherever it is quoted.

**Observation only.** Two claims built on top of this number are retracted: the
2.80 A this project restrained for five weeks is our own model's number and
appears in no deposited file, and ¶184's 2.68 A superposes 6NC5's zinc into a
metal-free structure (¶208 section 1).
