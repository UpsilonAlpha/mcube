# The beta-lactam carbonyl coordinates Zn1 in the Michaelis complex

The experimental anchor is L1's time-resolved Michaelis complexes 7UHI/7UHJ,
the only measured intact-substrate complex in the family: the carbonyl sits
**2.53/2.63 A** from Zn1 ([[l1-michaelis-complex-uses-both-zincs]]). That
withdrew D40's claim that inner-sphere coordination was unestablished (D123),
which is the bullet that had dropped the carbonyl restraint in the first place.

**Our own strongest datum for it is unrestrained, which is what makes it
interesting.** In QM/MM batch 1 the carbonyl started at 2.58 A - outside the
2.5 A bond radius - with nothing holding it, and spent 66% of 50 ps inside
2.5 A at 2.43 A mean, crossing the boundary in both directions (D200). That
sits on D124's loose-carbonyl precedent
([[thermolysin-and-cpa-hold-both-oxygens-cis-on-one-zinc]]: 2.41 and 2.98 A).

**Status is `open`, because the live alternative has never been excluded:**
that Zn1 polarises the carbonyl without binding it - the position D53 held and
D121/D123 withdrew - and because the geometry that follows from coordination
has not been made to work
([[question-can-burgi-dunitz-coexist-with-carbonyl-on-one-zinc]]). The docked
pose in use is also 5.15 A from L1's measured mode and never makes the
carboxylate-Arg contact every other MBL substrate complex shows (D156), so the
pose itself is the blocking scientific question (D89).
