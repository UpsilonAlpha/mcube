# The beta-lactam carbonyl coordinates Zn1 in the Michaelis complex

The experimental anchor is L1's time-resolved Michaelis complexes 7UHI/7UHJ,
the only measured intact-substrate complex in the family: the carbonyl sits
**2.53/2.63 A** from Zn1 ([[l1-michaelis-complex-uses-both-zincs]]). That
withdrew ¶40's claim that inner-sphere coordination was unestablished (¶123),
which is the bullet that had dropped the carbonyl restraint in the first place.

**Our own strongest datum for it is unrestrained, which is what makes it
interesting.** In QM/MM batch 1 the carbonyl started at 2.58 A - outside the
2.5 A bond radius - with nothing holding it, and spent 66% of 50 ps inside
2.5 A at 2.43 A mean, crossing the boundary in both directions (¶200). That
sits on ¶124's loose-carbonyl precedent
([[thermolysin-and-cpa-hold-both-oxygens-cis-on-one-zinc]]: 2.41 and 2.98 A).

**Status is `open`, because the live alternative has never been excluded:**
that Zn1 polarises the carbonyl without binding it - the position ¶53 held and
¶121/¶123 withdrew - and because the geometry that follows from coordination
has not been made to work
([[question-can-burgi-dunitz-coexist-with-carbonyl-on-one-zinc]]). The docked
pose in use is also 5.15 A from L1's measured mode and never makes the
carboxylate-Arg contact every other MBL substrate complex shows (¶156), so the
pose itself is the blocking scientific question (¶89).

**¶301 GIVES THIS ITS FIRST DIRECT EXPERIMENTAL MEASUREMENT, from the deposited
coordinates of the L1 time-resolved series** (7UHH-7UHL, moxalactam, intact
beta-lactam), computed from the PDB files rather than quoted from the paper:

    Zn1 - O(beta-lactam carbonyl)   2.53 (7UHI)  2.63 (7UHJ)  2.53 (7UHL)
    Zn2 - O(carbonyl)               4.09         4.07         4.02
    Zn2 - O(C-4 carboxylate)        2.65         2.54         ~2.5
    Zn2 - N(lactam)                 2.67         2.78          -
    7-alpha-methoxy O - Zn1         3.72         4.23          -

**The carbonyl is on Zn1, the 3H/M1 metal, and only on Zn1** - 4.0-4.1 Å from
Zn2 in all three - while the carboxylate and the leaving nitrogen are on Zn2.
No substituent oxygen is metal-bound. Caveat from the deposits: Zn1 occupancy
0.48-0.53, Zn2 0.73-0.77, ligand 0.80-0.83, so these are partially occupied
time-resolved snapshots.

**This raises the claim's support but does not settle it, because L1 has two
zincs and we have one.** What it establishes is that the carbonyl belongs to the
**M1 subsite**. For a mono-zinc enzyme the claim therefore inherits the prior
question of which subsite our metal occupies, and there is now a literature
rival on exactly that
([[a-mono-zinc-b3-carries-its-metal-in-the-dhh-site]]).
