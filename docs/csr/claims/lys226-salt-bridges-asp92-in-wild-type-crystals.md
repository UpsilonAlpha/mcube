# Lys226's ammonium salt-bridges Asp92's carboxylate in both wild-type CSR-1 crystals

Measured from the deposits, in BBL numbering: **Lys254 NZ - Asp120 OD 2.99 A in
6DN4** and **2.83 A in 6DQ2** (D209). Both are inside the range of a charged
hydrogen bond, and the pairing is present in both independent wild-type
structures.

The same contact survives into our own equilibrated model at **2.79 A**
(`eq6`, D214), where it was restrained at the structure's own distance - so the
model agrees with the crystal by construction there, and the crystal numbers
above are the evidence.

**Why this is the load-bearing measurement.** Where every canonical B3 structure
has a Zn2 ion holding this carboxylate at ~2.1 A
([[asp92-is-a-zn2-ligand-in-b3-structures]]), the wild-type enzyme has a lysine
ammonium holding it at 2.8-3.0 A. The project had recorded Lys226's position in
the Zn2 site since D22 but had **never recorded this contact** until D209 -
twelve weeks of treating the lysine as a spare cation near a vacancy rather than
as the thing occupying it.
