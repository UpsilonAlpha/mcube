# The non-truncated form we model is the catalytically relevant one

**An assertion, recorded as one.** CSR-1 is reported active only after
N-terminal truncation; in the mature full-length enzyme an N-terminal loop caps
the active site (D22). Our model is the **non-truncated** form.

The numbers that make it a live concern (D40): 6NC5 covers residues 15-273,
our model covers **1-275**, so residues 1-14 were modelled back in from no
experimental coordinates, and Ala12, Pro13, Tyr15, Leu17, Phe18, Glu19 and
Trp21 sit **9-13 A** from the zinc - nearer than anything from the crystal's
own N-terminus.

**What is known about the status of the inactivity claim:** D41 withdrew it as
stated - it was "relayed from a literature summary and not verified". So the
premise is not refuted either; it is simply unchecked in both directions.
`state.md` section 5 carries it as unresolved with the note "a referee will
ask".

**What would settle it.** Verify the truncation claim against Pedroso 2020
directly, and measure whether the modelled 1-14 segment occludes the site in
our own structures. Neither has been done. Until then no node downstream of
this one should be quoted without it.
