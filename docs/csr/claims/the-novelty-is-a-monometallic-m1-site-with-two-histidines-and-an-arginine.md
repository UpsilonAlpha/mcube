# The novel object is a monometallic site at M1 made of two histidines and an arginine, not monometallic catalysis as such

**The claim "a heretofore unreported B3 monometallic mechanism" is not
defensible** - GOB-18 is a mono-zinc B3 with a published mechanism
([[gob18-is-a-mono-zinc-b3-with-a-published-mechanism-at-zn2]]). Three narrower
clauses survive, and each is checkable:

1. **Site.** GOB-18's single metal is at **Zn2 (DHH)**; CSR-1's is at **M1**. No
   monometallic B3 mechanism at M1 is reported.
2. **Ligand set.** CSR-1's M1 is **two histidines plus Arg90**
   ([[csr1-zn1-site-is-h-r-h]]). No reported monometallic MBL mechanism operates
   at such a site: GOB's Zn1 carries Gln116, B1 mono-zinc BcII's site is three
   histidines ([[b1-monozinc-bcii-carries-its-metal-at-zn1]]), and B2 Sfh-I's
   resting site is one histidine plus one sulfur.
3. **Nucleophile.** A metal-activated nucleophile in a monometallic MBL is
   explicitly denied by the only published mono-zinc mechanism
   ([[the-published-mono-zinc-mechanism-has-no-metal-activated-nucleophile]]),
   so this clause is a **disagreement with a named paper** rather than a gap.

**Open**, because clause 3 is the one the project has been trying to establish
and has not: no reaction coordinate has been computed
([[no-reaction-coordinate-has-ever-been-computed]]) and there is no usable
Michaelis complex ([[no-usable-michaelis-complex-exists-yet]]).

**Clauses 1 and 2 are structural and nearly in hand.** Clause 2 in particular
needs no barrier - it is a statement about the ligand set of an occupied site,
and the one thing still missing is a wild-type structure with a metal in it
([[wild-type-csr1-binds-no-zinc-in-any-crystal]]).
