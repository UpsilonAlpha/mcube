# ITC puts the CSR-1 beta-site metal affinity ~75-fold weaker than the alpha site

Wild-type CSR-1 by isothermal titration calorimetry: alpha (Zn1) site
Kd ~ **2 uM**; beta (Zn2) site Kd ~ **150 uM** - weak, and not inhibitory
(¶22, from Pedroso 2020).

This is independent of the crystallography and points the same way: the second
site is not occupied under conditions where the first one is. It is also the
mechanism offered for why the wild-type crystals came out apo
([[wild-type-csr1-crystals-contain-no-metal]]) - Arg118 and Lys254 are
protonated at optimal pH, so neither site binds strongly.

**Evidence class `literature`, deliberately.** The ITC numbers are read from
the paper, not re-derived here, and no raw data has been inspected. Under ¶206
that places them below a deposited structure and above anything of ours.
