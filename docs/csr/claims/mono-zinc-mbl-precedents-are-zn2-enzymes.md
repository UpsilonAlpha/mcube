# Every mono-zinc MBL in the literature carries its metal at Zn2, so no published mono-zinc mechanism transfers to CSR-1

GOB-18's single metal is described at the DHH (Zn2) site; Sfh-I's is DCH-like;
B2 CphA's is Asp-Cys-His, and in B2 the His116Asn substitution removes a Zn1
ligand outright, so there nucleophile activation involves no metal at all
(¶124). GOB-18's published mechanism is a **non-metal-bound** water activated by
a hydrogen-bond network, with the metal binding the C-3 carboxylate and the
sphere expanding four- to five-coordinate on substrate binding (Lisa 2017).

**That is a description of the site CSR-1 leaves empty.** CSR-1's metal is at
Zn1 ([[csr1-zinc-sits-at-zn1]]), so GOB-18 says what CSR-1's *vacant* site
would have done, not what its occupied one does (¶107), and the CphA route "is
not a mechanism for a Zn1-site enzyme" (¶124).

This is the strongest single argument in the map that CSR-1's mechanism must
differ from anything published: there is no mono-zinc MBL template at the right
site to copy, and the di-zinc templates are missing the metal that holds the
part of the substrate nearest the chemistry
([[l1-michaelis-complex-uses-both-zincs]]).

**Demoted from `supported` to `open`, 2026-11-06.** Not because the argument is
weak - it is the strongest in the map - but because its load-bearing GOB-18
premise is a literature description that the deposit does not evidence, and the
node said so itself while claiming `supported`. What it needs is the Lisa 2017
paper read for its solution-state evidence, or the claim narrowed to Sfh-I and
CphA, which are unaffected.

**One by-product correction to carry:** the claim that GOB-18's single zinc
sits at Zn2 is not what the deposit shows
([[gob18-deposit-has-four-zincs]]). The literature description may still be
right about the solution species; the structure does not evidence it.

**REFUTED 2026-11-14 (¶265), by the counterexample the user named.**
Mono-zinc **BcII** (B1) carries its single metal at **Zn1 / 3H**, and Bounaga
et al. 1998 published a Zn1-Lewis-acid hydroxide mechanism for that species
([[b1-monozinc-bcii-carries-its-metal-at-zn1]]). So a published mono-zinc
mechanism at CSR-1 metal position does exist, and the node title is false as
written. The B1 subgroup was never checked - the node reasoned from B2 CphA,
GOB-18 and Sfh-I only.

**The narrow form that survives, and is still worth having:** no published
mono-zinc mechanism operates at a **two-histidine** Zn1 site. BcII Zn1 is
three histidines; CSR-1 is two plus an arginine. So the precedent transfers
the metal position and the nucleophile, not the ligand set - which is a
weaker claim than this node made and does not carry
[[thesis-mechanism-differs-from-canonical-b3]] on its own.
